INSTALL DCLUTTER
===============

1. Drag DClutter onto the Applications shortcut in this window.
   This copies the app to your Mac. Don't run it from the disk image.

2. Eject the DClutter disk image in Finder.

3. Open DClutter from Applications.
   If macOS shows "DClutter Not Opened", click Done.

4. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security,
   and click Open Anyway next to DClutter.

5. Confirm Open and authenticate if asked.
   After this one-time approval, open DClutter normally from Applications.

This build is not Developer ID signed or notarized. Packaging it as a DMG
does not change its signing status or remove the first-launch warning.
